Frequently Asked

Questions & Answers

What design services do you offer?+

I create logo and brand identity designs, social media graphics, posters, banners, packaging, and print-ready stationery.

How long does a project usually take?+

Simple projects usually take 3-5 days. Larger branding or multi-deliverable projects can take 1-2 weeks depending on scope and feedback.

How much does a design project cost?+

Pricing depends on the project type, deliverables, revisions, and complexity. Contact me with your requirements for a tailored quote.

How many revisions are included?+

The revision limit varies by package and is confirmed before work begins. I always aim to refine the direction until the approved result is right.

What information do you need to start?+

Please share your project goal, preferred style, brand colors, text, dimensions, deadline, and any reference designs you like.
